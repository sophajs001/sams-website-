Administration & Leadership

Our administration and leadership team guide the Seminary’s vision, ensuring excellence in formation, academics, and pastoral training for all seminarians.

From the Desk of the Rector

Welcome to St. Augustine’s Major Seminary, a distinguished centre of priestly, intellectual, and spiritual formation dedicated to the service of God and His Church. Rooted in the rich tradition of Catholic education, our seminary seeks to form men who are committed to holiness, academic excellence, pastoral charity, and faithful discipleship.

Inspired by our patron, St. Augustine, we embrace the harmonious pursuit of faith and reason, recognizing that true wisdom is found in the knowledge and love of God. Through a balanced programme of human, spiritual, intellectual, and pastoral formation, we prepare future priests to respond effectively to the challenges and opportunities of contemporary ministry.

Our seminary community is built upon prayer, study, fraternity, discipline, and service. Within this environment, seminarians are encouraged to grow in virtue, deepen their relationship with Christ, and acquire the knowledge and skills necessary for fruitful priestly ministry. Supported by a dedicated faculty and formation team, they are formed to become compassionate shepherds, capable leaders, and authentic witnesses of the Gospel.

As a centre of theological and philosophical learning, St. Augustine’s Major Seminary remains committed to fostering scholarship, critical inquiry, and a profound appreciation of the Church’s intellectual heritage. We strive to form men who will contribute meaningfully to the evangelizing mission of the Church and the transformation of society in the light of Christian values.

We invite you to learn more about our seminary, our programmes, and our mission. May this institution continue to serve as a beacon of faith, wisdom, and hope for the Church and the wider community.
